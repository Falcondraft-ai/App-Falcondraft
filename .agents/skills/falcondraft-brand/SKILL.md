FalconDraft is a premium agency/productized service brand that sells a complete, turnkey, personalized system for automating the creation of commercial proposals.

## Brand positioning

FalconDraft should feel:
- premium
- credible
- sharp
- structured
- strategic
- calm
- high-trust

It should NOT feel:
- cheap
- startup-generic
- overly playful
- futuristic gimmicky
- AI-template-like
- noisy

## Visual identity

Use the brand logo and its cues as guidance:
- deep navy as primary structural color
- restrained gold/copper accent
- clear document / precision / control symbolism
- elegant and serious composition

## UI direction

Prefer:
- editorial layouts
- strong typography
- generous whitespace
- crisp lines and clean surfaces
- restrained shadows
- premium rhythm
- asymmetry used with control

Avoid:
- generic SaaS cards everywhere
- random gradients
- icon spam
- shiny AI tropes
- flashy animations
- cluttered sections

## Brand tone

The site should communicate:
- mastery
- operational clarity
- speed with rigor
- custom-fit automation
- business credibility

The result must look like a real high-end agency website, not an AI-generated landing page.
