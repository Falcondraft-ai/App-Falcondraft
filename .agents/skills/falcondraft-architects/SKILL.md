FalconDraft primarily targets architecture firms and similar businesses with complex proposals.

## Positioning for architects

The site must reflect the realities of architecture firms:
- responses to calls for tender
- project-specific adaptation
- multiple document variants
- reuse of references and methodologies
- need for precision and consistency
- commercial documents that must feel credible and controlled

## Messaging priorities

Emphasize:
- precision
- rigor
- repeatability
- time saving
- quality consistency
- adaptation to each project or client
- reducing dependence on copy-paste workflows

## Avoid

- generic startup messaging
- vague automation jargon
- overly technical AI language
- language that ignores sector-specific document realities

## Content angle

When writing copy or structuring sections, bias toward:
- project complexity
- commercial clarity
- proposal quality
- operational reliability

The audience should feel:
"this was built for firms like ours."
