FalconDraft uses storytelling as the core structure of the website.

## Main narrative

The site tells the transition from:
manual, fragmented, inconsistent proposal creation

to:
a controlled, personalized, turnkey proposal system

## Narrative arc

Always structure the page around these beats:

1. Hook
FalconDraft automates and personalizes proposal creation for companies that need to respond faster and with more consistency.

2. Tension
Commercial proposals are often slow, fragmented, repetitive, and dependent on manual effort.

3. Deeper problem
The real issue is not only time loss:
- quality varies
- knowledge is scattered
- teams depend on a few people
- proposals do not consistently reflect the firm's value

4. Transformation
FalconDraft designs a complete, custom system:
- structured content
- reusable logic
- automation
- personalization
- operational clarity

5. Proof / outcome
The company can produce proposals faster, more cleanly, and more consistently.

6. CTA
Move from artisanal proposal work to a controlled proposal system.

## Rules

Before generating sections:
- identify where each narrative beat appears
- ensure each section advances the story
- avoid stacking generic marketing blocks
- prioritize progression, not brochure logic

## Tone

The storytelling should feel:
- strategic
- precise
- business-oriented
- premium
- confident

Never:
- overhype
- sound vague
- sound like generic AI automation marketing
